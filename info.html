How to Watch

Everything you need to start streaming. Your Wycoff Media username and password work everywhere.

🌐

Web Browser

The easiest way to get started — no app needed.

Jellyfin (Watch)

Open any browser — Chrome, Firefox, or Safari — and go to:

jellyfin.wycoffcomm.com

Sign in with your Wycoff Media username and password.

Jellyseerr (Request Content)

Want to request a movie or show? Visit:

seerr.wycoffcomm.com

Same username and password as Jellyfin.

📱

Phone & Tablet

Download the Jellyfin app, then point it at the server.

iPhone & iPad

Search Jellyfin in the App Store, or use Infuse for a more polished experience.

Server: jellyfin.wycoffcomm.com

Android

Search Jellyfin in the Google Play Store.

Server: jellyfin.wycoffcomm.com
📺

TV & Streaming Devices

Install the Jellyfin app, enter the server address, and sign in.

Server address for all apps: jellyfin.wycoffcomm.com
🔴
Roku Search "Jellyfin" in the Roku Channel Store
🔥
Amazon Fire TV / Stick Search "Jellyfin" in the Amazon Appstore
🍎
Apple TV Download Infuse or Jellyfin from the App Store
🤖
Android TV / Google TV Search "Jellyfin" in the Google Play Store
🅽
Nvidia Shield Install Jellyfin from Google Play
🎮
Xbox Open the browser and go to jellyfin.wycoffcomm.com, or search "Jellyfin" in the Microsoft Store
🎮
PlayStation Open the browser and go to jellyfin.wycoffcomm.com
📺
Samsung / LG Smart TV Search for Jellyfin in the built-in app store. If unavailable, use the TV's browser.
